- The published data archives described in [`DATA.md`](DATA.md): the packet captures
  (stripped to packet headers, with no payload bytes), their JSON label sidecars, and the
  extracted feature matrices.
- The result files under `data/results/` (JSON, Markdown tables and figures).

## Provenance

All traffic in the dataset was generated by the authors on infrastructure they own; no third
party's traffic was captured. See "Provenance & ethics" in [`DATA.md`](DATA.md).
